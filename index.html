Welcome to Tahí
Woven by Stories

Rooted in the rich culture of San Lorenzo, Guimaras, Tahi is a social enterprise that showcases the craftsmanship of the local artisans from the People's Handicraft and Processed Food Center in Brgy. Sapal. Each product tells a personal story—stitched by hand and infused with heart.

About Us

Mission and Vision

Founded on the Hiligaynon word “Tahi” meaning “to sew,” we are a movement dedicated to preserving culture, empowering artisans, and reshaping commerce with ethical storytelling.

  • Mission: To empower local communities by providing sustainable livelihood opportunities through ethical storytelling commerce and preserving our local weaving heritage.
  • Vision: We envision a world where local weaving traditions are honored, celebrated, and sustained—and where every artisan’s cultural pride is stitched into every home through meaningful products.

Meet the Artisans

Sa sulod sang apat ka dekada, ginpangabuhi ko ang paghimo sang banig, bags, kag iba pa nga produkto. Indi lang ni obra, isa ini ka testamento sang kabuhi sang mga babayi pareho ko nga may gahum, talento, kag kusog. Kabay pa nga ma-appreciate sang mga kabataan ang ginikanan sang aton mga produkto. For four decades, I’ve lived a life of crafting mats, bags, and other items. This is not just a livelihood, it is a testament to the strength, skill, and power of women like me. I hope the youth will come to appreciate where these products come from.

Name: Rosalina “Lina” Flores Mendoza
Age: 52 years old
Years in Crafting: 28 ka tuig (28 years)
Personal Mantra: “Biskan gamay ang obra, basta halin sa tagipusuon.”
(“Even the smallest work matters, as long as it's made from the heart.”)

Name: Isabel “Belen” Ramos Castor
Age: 64 years old
Years in Crafting: 40 ka tuig (40 years)
Personal Mantra: “Ang kultura, indi pagkalimtan, ipakita sa aton mga obra.”
(“Let culture never be forgotten, show it through our work.”)

Halos kabuhi ko na ang pagpanahi kag pagbanig. Indi gid matupungan ang kalipay nga ginahatag sang sini nga obra. Sa sini nga edad, may ara ako gihapon kabakod kag kalipay nga magpadayon. Gina-panawagan ko sa mga kabataan, kilalaha kag pasidunggi ang inyo ugat. I’ve spent almost my whole life sewing and weaving. The joy it brings is unmatched. Even at this age, I still find strength and happiness in this work. I urge the youth, know and honor your roots.

Name: Benedicta “Nida” Tolentino Gonzaga

Personal Mantra: “Sa katahum sang obra, may katahum man sang kabuhi.”
(“In the beauty of our work lies the beauty of life.”)


Pisan nga Kamot, Isa ka Komunidad

(Diligent Hands, One Community)


Sa Sapal: Kultura, Kabuhi, kag Pagbinuligay

Sa Sapal, ang paghimo sang banig kag handwoven crafts indi lang trabaho—isa ini ka kabuhi, isa ka panulundan, kag isa ka pag-ugyon. Diri nagatipon ang mga iloy, lola, kag kababaihan sang komunidad nga may parehong handum: ang pagpadayon sang kultura, kag ang pagpanikasog para sa ila pamilya.

Edukasyon

Training sa traditional weaving para sa oportunidad kag kultura.

Marketing

Suporta sa promotion kag pagpakilala sang produkto.

Lugar

May espasyo sila nga luwas kag makapalapad sang relasyon.

Pagpasidungog

Ginkilala sila bilang artista kag bantay sang kultura.
